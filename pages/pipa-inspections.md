      ## What is PIPA Testing?

      PIPA is an annual inspection scheme for health and safety endorsed by the HSE (Health and Safety Executive). The scheme ensures that inflatable play equipment is regularly inspected, properly maintained, and safe for public use.

      Our inspections follow the EU standard **BS EN 14960**, which sets out the safety requirements and test methods for inflatable play equipment. All operators of inflatable play equipment used for commercial purposes require annual PIPA inspections to comply with health and safety regulations.

      ## Equipment We Inspect

      The PIPA scheme covers a growing list of inflatable play equipment, and our PIPA inspection service covers everything within its scope, including:

      - Bouncy castles, A-frame and enclosed units
      - Combination bounce-and-slide units, inflatable slides and water slides
      - Assault courses and obstacle runs
      - Disco domes and snowglobes
      - Bungee runs and bouncing pillows
      - Ball pits and toddler play zones — added to the scheme's scope for the 2026 season
      - Non ride-on games such as batak walls, football shootouts, archery and axe-throwing targets, and side stalls

      Some equipment still falls outside PIPA's remit — pole jousts, gladiator duels, human table football, pool inflatables and sealed-air equipment, for example. We inspect these annually as a competent person and issue an in-house inspection report, keeping you compliant with health and safety law. You can check the [current scope of the scheme on the PIPA website](https://www.pipa.org.uk/about-us/scope-of-scheme/).
